EXECUTIVE MITIGATION: NASA SBIR REVIEWER RED-FLAG RESOLUTION

The following section directly resolves the top technical vulnerabilities identified by NASA fluid-mechanics and propulsion review panels regarding the original conceptual demonstrator:

PREVIOUS RED FLAG #1 ENGINEERED FIX

The "Magic Sensor" Density Problem

Solution: We do not assume a mythical monolithic point sensor. Intake void-fraction and bulk density ($\rho$) are measured via dual-modality: High-Frequency Radio Frequency Mass Gauging (RFMG) coupled with an annular Electrical Capacitance Tomography (ECT) ring installed flush on the engine sump inlet collar. This eliminates optical drift and provides microsecond dielectric permittivity measurement across LOX and $LH_2$ transitions.

PREVIOUS RED FLAG #2 ENGINEERED FIX

CFD vs. Flight Avionics Execution

Solution: Full Navier-Stokes 3D volume-of-fluid micro-boil CFD is prohibitively slow for in-situ flight. Sentinel deploys a Reduced-Order Model (ROM) Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) pre-trained on open NASA Glenn CFM test stand datasets. The ROM solves modal slosh harmonics and lumped boundary layer film superheat equations at >50 Hz on flight-qualified rad-hard processors (e.g., RAD750 or Vorago ARM Cortex).

PREVIOUS RED FLAG #3 ENGINEERED FIX

GN&C Attitude Control Destabilization

Solution: Settling burns cannot be commanded blindly without upsetting spacecraft pointing. Sentinel features a Coupled Torque-Balanced GN&C Arbiter. Pulsed settling thruster vectors are co-synthesized with spacecraft reaction wheels and opposed thruster pairs, guaranteeing net zero unwanted moment ($\Sigma M = 0$) during high-priority orbital burns.

PREVIOUS RED FLAG #4 ENGINEERED FIX

Empirical Validation Pathway

Solution: Phase I terminates not in simple front-end graphics, but in empirical drop-tower test validation (2.2s / 5.2s at NASA GRC Zero-Gravity Facility) using cryogenic simulant fluid (Novec 1230 & Liquid Nitrogen $LN_2$) inside a transparent scaled tank equipped with the prototype ECT ring.
